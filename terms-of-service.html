Terms of Service — ConnectPost

Last updated: November 14, 2025

Welcome to ConnectPost. These Terms of Service explain how the ConnectPost app may be used inside my community Discord server.

1. Purpose

ConnectPost is a small community app designed for use in a Discord server.

Its purpose is to let members view publicly available TikTok profiles and publicly available TikTok videos directly inside Discord.

2. Allowed Use

  • You may use the app only within the Discord server where it is installed.
  • Usage is limited to personal and non-commercial purposes.
  • By using the app, you agree to follow these Terms and all applicable TikTok policies.

3. Data Usage

ConnectPost does not collect, store, save, or share:

  • personal data
  • private TikTok information
  • login credentials
  • user identifiers

The app retrieves only public TikTok content that is already available to anyone on TikTok without authentication.

All content is fetched in real time only to respond to a user's request and is not stored after it is displayed.

4. User Responsibility

  • Users are responsible for ensuring that the content they request or share through the app complies with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • ConnectPost does not modify TikTok content and does not verify the accuracy or availability of content.

5. Limitations

The app operates with strict limitations:

  • No scraping
  • No automation
  • No mass data collection
  • No access to private TikTok data
  • No posting actions
  • No login or OAuth authentication
  • No analytics or tracking

ConnectPost is strictly a display tool for publicly accessible information.

6. Changes

These Terms may be updated in the future if the app's functionality changes or if new requirements arise from Discord or TikTok.

7. Contact

For any questions about these Terms of Service, please contact the Discord server owner.
