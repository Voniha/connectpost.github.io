Privacy Policy

Data Collection

ConnectPost collects and stores no personal data from users. We do not require registration, login, or any form of authentication.

TikTok Content

ConnectPost only fetches publicly available TikTok data temporarily to display content within Discord. This data includes:

  • Public TikTok profile information
  • Public TikTok videos
  • Publicly available metadata

All fetched data is handled temporarily and is not stored permanently on our servers.

Data Storage

ConnectPost does not store any data beyond temporary caching necessary for displaying content. No user data, TikTok content, or any other information is permanently stored.

Third-Party Services

ConnectPost operates through Discord and displays content from TikTok. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these platforms:

  • Discord Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
